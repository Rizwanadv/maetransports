About Us

Building a legacy of reliable transportation across America and Canada.

Your Trusted Partner on the Open Road

MAE Legacy Transports LLC is a premier transportation company specializing in car carriers and container carriers. Based in Summerfield, Florida, we move vehicles, shipping containers, and general freight across the entire United States and into Canada.

With a fleet of approximately 500 trucks, we have the capacity and reach to handle large-scale shipments while maintaining the personal service and reliability that smaller fleets pride themselves on.

Whether you need to transport a single vehicle or an entire fleet of containers, our experienced drivers and modern equipment ensure your cargo arrives safely and on time. Safety, professionalism, and on-time delivery are at the core of everything we do.
